AI FINGERPRINT EXPERIMENT — TRAINING PERFORMANCE SCHEMA v1.1
============================================================

Scope
-----
These files are produced only for federated training when performance_logging.enabled=true. They are ground-truth/system-characterization artifacts and are forbidden as proxy fingerprinting predictors.

Client: round_metrics.csv
-------------------------
One row per completed client round.

Identity/context:
experiment_id, client_id, round, family, architecture, variant, application,
dataset, framework, runtime, precision, input_size, batch_size

Server-authoritative FL policy context:
global_rounds, local_epochs, steps_per_epoch, learning_rate,
training_policy_id, policy_source

Local training:
local_steps, train_samples,
train_loss, train_accuracy, train_precision, train_recall, train_f1,
train_reconstruction_loss, train_mse, train_mae, train_kl_loss, vae_beta

Round probe:
train_loss_before, train_loss_after, loss_change,
accuracy_before, accuracy_after, accuracy_change,
f1_before, f1_after, f1_change,
mse_before, mse_after, mse_change,
round_probe_samples, metric_source

Timing/communication:
download_time_sec, training_time_sec, upload_time_sec, sync_wait_time_sec,
transaction_time_sec, model_size_bytes

Model geometry:
global_model_norm_l2, local_model_norm_l2, update_norm_l2

Timestamps:
timestamp_start_utc, timestamp_end_utc

Server: client_update_metrics.csv
---------------------------------
One row per received client update per round.

experiment_id, round, client_id, num_examples, payload_bytes,
client_loss, client_accuracy, client_precision, client_recall, client_f1,
client_reconstruction_loss, client_mse, client_mae, client_kl_loss, vae_beta,
client_model_norm_l2, client_update_norm_l2, receive_timestamp_utc

Server: round_metrics.csv
-------------------------
One row per completed FedAvg aggregation.

Identity/context:
experiment_id, round, family, architecture, variant, application, dataset,
framework, runtime, aggregation_rule

Server-authoritative FL policy context:
input_size, batch_size, learning_rate, global_rounds, local_epochs,
steps_per_epoch, training_policy_id, policy_source

Participation:
clients_expected, clients_received, client_ids, total_examples

Global learning performance:
global_loss, global_accuracy, global_precision, global_recall, global_f1,
global_reconstruction_loss, global_mse, global_mae, global_kl_loss, vae_beta,
evaluation_samples, evaluation_split

Aggregation/model geometry:
aggregation_time_ms, evaluation_time_ms,
global_model_norm_l2, global_update_norm_l2,
mean_client_update_norm_l2, std_client_update_norm_l2, model_size_bytes

Per-round traffic/system timing:
bytes_received_round, bytes_sent_round, round_duration_sec

Convergence deltas:
loss_change (positive means loss improved),
accuracy_change (positive means accuracy improved),
f1_change (positive means F1 improved)

Timestamps/errors:
timestamp_start_utc, timestamp_end_utc, evaluation_error
